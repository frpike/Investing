The plan

Five stages from "never invested" to "running on autopilot". Tick things off as you go; your progress is saved.

The rules you'll stick to

  1. Keep investing every day, whatever the news says. Buying through falls is the whole point of the method.
  2. Only invest money you won't need for at least 5 years.
  3. At least 80% goes in a cheap global index fund. Individual companies live in the 20% "explore" slice, never more.
  4. Check in once a week when your reminder arrives, log it here, then close the app. Checking every day leads to bad decisions.
  5. Never sell because prices dropped. Only sell because your life needs the money or your plan changed.
  6. Raise the daily amount when your income goes up, even by £1.
